Model M RT: Standard Volatility Market Minute Buckets

Buckets are based on elapsed market minute, where source minute_number = 0 is displayed as Minute 1. Minute 1 and minute 30 are isolated single-minute views; the 10-minute buckets are inclusive elapsed-market ranges such as 1-10, 11-20, and so on. These bucket views intentionally overlap for isolated minute 1 and minute 30.
