Model D RT: High Volatility Extreme

Forecasts are minute-level Model D YES probabilities from the hourly forecast files. Reality is the official Kalshi contract result for the exact matched market, encoded as 1 for YES resolved and 0 for NO resolved.

Assumptions

  • Model D forecast values are interpreted as YES probabilities. CSV values greater than 1 and no greater than 100 are treated as cents and divided by 100 before scoring.
  • Scored event outcome is the official Kalshi contract result: 1 for YES resolved, 0 for NO resolved.
  • Forecast rows are joined to outcomes by exact Kalshi market ticker when available; legacy price files without market tickers fall back to event_ticker + contract_label + strike.
  • Event tickers are interpreted as the contract settlement/end hour, matching the price fetcher's hour_end_utc -> New York event ticker convention.
  • Binance prices are diagnostic only. They may be used for strike matching upstream and audit mismatch checks, but never as the scored truth label.
  • Events present in the Kalshi price files but absent from the settlement CSV are excluded from scoring and counted in outcome_join_coverage.csv.
  • Monte Carlo self-metric error estimates treat each forecast probability as a finite-path binomial estimate with variance p(1-p)/N, where N is the configured --mc-trials. Brier and log-loss uncertainty use first-order propagation, and classification accuracy uses a normal threshold-crossing approximation.
